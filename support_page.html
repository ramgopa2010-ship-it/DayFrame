This page focuses on providing support and contact information of organizations and individuals you can use. When you feel like you're alone, overwhelmed, or unsafe, you can contact the organizations mentioned here.

Proper Guidance & Support

You don’t have to handle this alone. Here are safe next steps.

Talk to Someone You Trust

Speak to a parent, teacher, mentor, or trusted adult. Opening up is often the first step toward relief.

School-Based Support

Contact your school office to schedule a counselling session. School professionals are trained to help confidentially.

National Helplines (India)

KIRAN: 1800-599-0019

Immediate Help

If you feel unsafe or overwhelmed beyond control, contact emergency services immediately.

When to Reach Out

If any of these feel true, support can really help.

  • Stress is affecting your sleep or appetite for several days.
  • You feel unsafe, threatened, or targeted.
  • You’re avoiding school or certain places because of fear.
  • You’re having frequent panic/anxiety moments.
  • You feel hopeless, numb, or like you can’t cope.

Quick Words You Can Use

Copy a line. Send it. That’s enough to start.

To a Parent / Guardian

“I’ve been feeling overwhelmed lately. Can we talk for a few minutes?”

To a Teacher

“I’m struggling and I’m not sure what to do. Can you guide me to the right support?”

To a Friend

“I don’t need advice right now. I just need someone to listen.”

If You Feel Unsafe

“I’m not feeling safe right now. I need help immediately.”

Privacy Note

This page does not collect personal data. If you choose to reach out, use the support option that feels safest for you. If you are in danger, contact emergency services immediately.
